Dr Phil's Math Blog Interactive tools for seeing the proof

Math you can drag around.

Most tools here turn a theorem into something you can move with your hands. Drag the shapes, watch the invariants hold, and see for yourself why the proof works, not just read that it does. A few entries are flat writeups where the argument speaks for itself.

Curve Lengths

Watch the curves x to the n converge to a step shape while their arc length climbs to exactly 2, a clean example of why pointwise limits do not always carry length along with them.

Open the tool →

a to the b equals b to the a

A full proof that the only natural-number solutions to a^b = b^a are the trivial pairs and the single pair (2, 4), shown two ways: a calculus argument and a purely arithmetical one.
