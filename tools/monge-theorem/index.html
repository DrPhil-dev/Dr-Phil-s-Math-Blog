The theorem

For any three circles with distinct radii, the three external centers of similitude — the points where a common external tangent line pair for each ordered pair of circles converge — always lie on one straight line, the axis of similitude. Each internal center of similitude also lies on a line with two external centers, showing the four-line concurrency structure behind Monge's theorem.
